The thesis

Attention is a commodity. Trust is the only true currency.

Every network sells you attention — a room full of people who might, one day, need what you do.

Trust is what actually moves work between businesses, and trust doesn’t come from a membership tier. It comes from showing up, being useful, and doing what you said.

What the fee does to people

A monthly fee turns curiosity into obligation. And obligation is what people fake.

We ran a paid membership network for years. It worked. Real introductions, real deals, real partnerships.

And underneath it, slowly, the thing that makes people collaborate — genuine interest in each other — wore down into a calendar entry. Attendance became a chore. Conversations became transactions. The fee that was supposed to signal commitment ended up manufacturing the opposite.

It got tedious.
So we did away with it.
